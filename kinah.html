Earning Kinah

AION 2 has two kinds of Kinah, and most early income is the kind you can't trade. Knowing which is which tells you where to spend your time. Tradeable Kinah is also what you use to buy Quna on the exchange for Quna skins.

Bound vs tradeable

Bound Kinah

Comes from quests, sealed dungeons, Duty Missions and selling to NPC vendors. Pays for enhancement and other upgrades, but can't be used on the Market.

Tradeable Kinah

Comes from other players buying your items, Conquest and Transcendence rewards, and monster drops. This is the Kinah you need for the Market and the Quna exchange.

When you pay for upgrades, the game spends bound Kinah first. So spend bound Kinah freely on enhancement and keep tradeable Kinah for buying.

A daily routine

  1. Duty Missions

    50,000 bound Kinah each. Rerolling a mission gets expensive fast, so stop once a reroll costs more than a few thousand.

  2. Conquest runs with your Odyle Energy

    The highest Conquest you can clear reliably. Spend energy before it hits the cap; anything over the cap is wasted. Keep tradeable Wrathful materials from Fire Temple and Horn Den.

  3. Gather along the way

    Pick up ore, gem and Odyle nodes you pass. Keep high-grade (Pure, Radiant) materials to sell; sell only common overflow to vendors.

  4. Process before selling

    Turning gems, logs and Spiritstones into processed materials often sells for more than the raw items.

  5. Check the Market before you list

    Prices are set by players. Price to sell, not to sit. Look up whether you'll need a material yourself before selling it.

  6. Abyss when energy runs out

    Once you have the gear to survive it, the Abyss gives Kinah and Abyss Points without using Odyle Energy.

Good to know

  • Buying from the Market and using the Quna exchange need the membership. Free players can still list items for sale.
  • Alts don't double server-wide limits. Duty Missions are shared by every character on the same server.
  • "Kina" and "Kinah" are the same currency. The global client spells it Kina.
  • Don't buy or sell Kinah for real money. NCSOFT restricted nearly 5,000 global accounts for gold farming and RMT on October 10, and warned that buyers can be banned too.
