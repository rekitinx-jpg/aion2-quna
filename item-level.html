Item Level roadmap

Item level is the total of your equipped gear and what's slotted into it. Content opens at fixed item levels, so the fastest route is to aim for the next threshold, not to max one piece. Enter your item level below to see what's open and what's next.

The roadmap, stage by stage

  1. Level 45 → 1,000 item level

    Finish the story and the rune quests

    • Complete the main story for a Unique weapon, plus the guard and bracelet from the last Ascension and Abyss quests.
    • Do the two side quests that give the Clash Rune chest.
    • Fill empty slots with elite set pieces from your home maps. Don't put good Manastones on them; you'll replace them.
  2. 1,000 → 1,400

    Cheap, guaranteed upgrades

    • Clear green side quests and sealed dungeons for Daevanion points.
    • Morph the Noble Belt and Revelation Amulet up to Unique (item level 65 each).
    • Enhance weapon, guard and bracelet to +10, which can't fail, and add a Manastone line to each.
    • Enhance both Clash Runes to +1.
    • Quest gear alone is reported to reach about 1,400.
  3. 1,400 → 2,100

    Vakron and Transcendence

    • Spend Odyle Energy on Vakron Sky Island for Unique armor at 70, or craft Dragon Lord pieces for slots that won't drop.
    • Enhance new pieces to about +5–7 and slot Manastones.
    • At 1,600, start Transcendence and fill Arcana slots. This is the biggest jump in this stage.
  4. 2,100 → 2,800

    Horn Den, Fire Temple and Amplify

    • Farm Ferocious Horn Den for Nuakum armor (86) and Fire Temple for accessories.
    • Use Gear Change Vouchers for missing slots, guard first.
    • Amplify your Unique pieces (+5 per level).
    • From here, put more into Combat Power (Soul Binding, better Manastones); see the Combat Power guide.

  • Gear never breaks. A failed attempt costs the stones and Kinah, and the item keeps its current level.
  • Failures build pity. Each failure adds about 5% to the next try at that level.
  • Costs climb steeply. On item level 102 gold gear, one +10 → +11 attempt is about 360,000 Kinah and 9,310 stones.
  • The +16 to +20 stage opens on gold gear from item level 54 and uses its own material.
  • Clash Runes are the exception. A failed rune enhancement can lower its level. Enhance a spare rune, then swap it in.

Morph, Transfer and old gear

Substance Morph

For the amulet and belt. Enhance to +10, take the piece off, then morph it in the Cube to the next grade. It always succeeds and resets to +0. Go Rare → Epic → Unique, then stop enhancing.

Gear Transfer

Moves enhancement and Soul Binding onto a new piece of the same type. Manastones, Theostones and Potential don't move. You can't transfer from a higher grade to a lower one. Worth it only for heavily enhanced pieces.

Old gear

Extracting returns Enhance Stones and unlocks the look in your Wardrobe, but destroys slotted Manastones. Keep pieces you plan to transfer and gold pieces with good Soul Binding. Otherwise hand them to Supply Requests.

Sources disagree on a few details, such as whether +16 to +20 transfers and whether Manastone rerolls let you lock slots. Check the in-game window before you confirm.
