Abyss Points

Abyss Points (AP) aren't only for PvP players. The Abyss shop sells Stigma Shards and Daevanion Crystals, which are permanent power for everyone. Several AP sources don't need any PvP at all.

Enter at level 45 + ~1,000 item levelDying doesn't cost AP

What AP buys

Stigma Shards

Level your Stigmas. Sold in four tiers of 50 shards, reported at 10,000 / 15,000 / 20,000 / 25,000 AP. Opens at level 45 in the shop's Growth tab.

Daevanion Crystals

Permanent Daevanion points: Combat Power and item level at the same time.

PvP gear and runes

Worth it once you play the Abyss regularly. Buy Shards and Crystals first.

Don't mix up Abyss Points and Daevanion points. Check the currency icon in the shop before you buy.

The Abyss, sieges and the Corridor

Getting in

Lower Reshanta opens at level 45 and about 1,000 item level. Time inside is limited by weekly Abyss hours (reported around 7 per week), so plan your sessions.

Artifact sieges

Three artifacts sit on the Reshanta map; the faction that lands the last hit on a core captures it. Each artifact you hold gives your whole faction an AP bonus (reported +2.5% per artifact). One guide lists sieges on Monday, Thursday and Saturday at 21:00 server time; NCSOFT hasn't confirmed a schedule.

Abyss Corridor

An instance your faction unlocks by holding artifacts, one entry per artifact. Entries expire at the next siege, so use them early. Monsters respawn nonstop: ranged classes stay in the middle with area attacks, melee keeps the entrance packs spawning.

Ranks

Your Abyss rank compares your Rank Points against other players of your own class. Ranks go from Trainee through nine Soldier tiers, five Officer tiers, General, Marshal and Commander up to Supreme Commander at 20,000,000 Rank Points, which gives a 5% PvP damage bonus.

  • On global, rank gives a PvP bonus only, no extra AP.
  • Dying costs Rank Points, not Abyss Points, so PvE players lose nothing they spend.

Tips

  • PvE players should still visit. Duty Missions, Supply Requests and the Corridor give AP with little or no PvP.
  • Spend AP on Stigma Shards and Daevanion Crystals first.
  • Play at off-peak hours if you're outgeared, and stay with your faction during sieges.
  • Don't use bots, auto-hunt macros or third-party tools. NCSOFT is banning for them.
