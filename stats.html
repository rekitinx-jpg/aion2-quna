Stats & best-in-slot

What every stat actually does, the caps that make extra points worthless, which stats each class should chase, and the best gear for every slot right now.

Caps to know

StatCapWhat it means
Critical Hit rate50%On the character sheet, before the target's Critical Hit Resist. Stop stacking Critical Hit once you're there; class buffs can push past it in combat.
Evasion rate30%Extra Evasion beyond this does nothing.
Cooldown reduction60%From the Illusion stat, Daevanion, titles, wings, Pantheon and Arcana.
MP cost reduction60%From the Wisdom stat.
Combat SpeedbreakpointsSmall amounts may change nothing; hitting a breakpoint speeds up your rotation. Test before investing.

How Manastone damage lines convert: a 100-point line equals 1% of that Boost stat. Double Chance (called Smite by many players) can't roll on Manastones; on global it comes from the Special Genus Insight board, Daevanion and the Wisdom stat.

Before 86: the 70 tier

At 1,400 item level, Vakron Sky Island drops Unique armor and guard at 70, and Urugugu Canyon drops the Aulamus weapon and accessories at 70. Drop rates are low (about 0.4–1.7% per item), so use Gear Change Vouchers to turn unwanted pieces into the slot you need; guaranteed rewards come every 21 claims.

Crafted vs dropped

Crafted Dragon Lord accessories start at 62, or 70 with a lucky Splendent result (25% chance per step), so the 70 dungeon accessories are usually better until you can craft the top tiers. Guides report crafted gear also has more Soul Binding slots; check the item tooltip before you commit materials.

What carries over: enhancement and Soul Binding move to the next item through Gear Transfer. Manastones and Soulstones don't, so they're lost when you replace a piece.

Combat statWhat it does
Double Chance ("Smite")Chance for a hit to deal double, based on final damage. One of the strongest damage stats; returns shrink as you stack it.
Perfect ChanceChance for a hit to use your weapon's maximum attack.

PenetrationIgnores part of the target's Defense (it can't drop below 0).
AccuracyFewer misses, blocks and parries. Raise it only if your big hits are being avoided.
EnduranceChance to halve damage you take. Endurance Penetration counters it.
