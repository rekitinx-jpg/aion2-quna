Nightmare

Nightmare is a solo boss ladder that opens at level 45. You fight one boss at a time, each with 10 challenge levels, and earn Phantasmal Fragments to spend in the Nightmare shop. Its best rewards are permanent power: Daevanion Crystals, Stigma Shards and Clash Runes.

2 attempts a dayBank up to 14Failed fights are free

How attempts work

Only a kill costs an attempt

If you wipe, you keep the attempt. Use failed pulls to learn the fight for free.

2 per day, up to 14 stored

You can skip a few days and catch up, but at 14 new attempts are lost. Spend some before you hit the cap.

Careful with skipping levels

Guides disagree on whether jumping to a higher challenge level also pays the first-clear rewards of the levels you skipped. Until that's confirmed, clearing levels in order is the safe way to collect every first-clear reward.

The bosses

Item level is the suggested range from challenge level 1 to 10. Clear the four common bosses first; their progress unlocks the rare ones, which then unlock Zikel.

What to buy in the Nightmare shop

  1. Daevanion Crystals: 300 each

    Permanent Daevanion points, which add both Combat Power and item level. Best value while your board isn't full.

  2. Stigma Shards: 1,500 each

    Level the Stigmas you use. Reported limit of 35.

  3. Clash Rune Chest: 500

    Each Clash Rune is +40 item level. Buy one if a rune slot is empty or weak.

  4. Long-term: statues and wings

    Zikel's Apparition Pantheon statue costs 14,000. Conqueror Wings (about 20,000) and Nightmare Wings (about 160,000) are big savings goals. Wings and statues add Combat Power through collections.

Most shop items have weekly or one-time limits. Check them before the Wednesday reset.

Tips

  • Enhance to +10 first. It can't fail and it's the cheapest damage you can add before a push.
  • Use your boss build, not your leveling or farming build.
  • Bring consumables for Zikel: HP potions, cleanse potions and food.
  • Save Battle Enhance Scrolls for the boss level you're stuck on.
  • If you die with lots of boss HP left, work on the mechanics before chasing more damage.
  • Don't confuse it with the Ascension Trial. That's a separate set of dungeons with its own entries.
