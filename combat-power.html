Raise your Combat Power

Combat Power (CP) is the game's estimate of how strong your character really is. Gear is only part of it. Most players who feel stuck have high item level and half-empty side systems, and those side systems are usually the cheapest CP you can get.

Combat Power vs Item Level

Item Level

Adds up your equipped gear: base item level, enhancement, Manastones, runes and Arcana. Dungeons and raids use it as an entry requirement. It decides what you can enter.

Combat Power

Counts your actual stats from every system, including Daevanion, Stigmas, skills, pets and collections. It decides how fast you clear and how long you survive.

Same gear, very different strength. One reported comparison: a character at about 1,945 item level had roughly 90,000 CP, while another at 2,096 item level had only about 80,000. The second player had higher gear but empty Daevanion, Stigma and pet progress.

The order to upgrade in

Free systems first, then the pieces you keep longest. Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. Spend everything that's free

    Daevanion points, skill points and Stigma Shards cost no gear drops and no luck. Empty these every time you log in.

  2. Weapon to +10

    Your main weapon gives the most CP per enhancement. Every attempt up to +10 succeeds, so the only cost is materials.

  3. Amulet and belt: +10, then Substance Morph

    Take the piece off, morph it to the next grade (Rare, then Epic, then Unique), and enhance it again. At Unique each is item level 65. Morph resets enhancement to +0, so stop enhancing once it's Unique.

  4. Start pets early

    Genus Insight builds up slowly, so the sooner you start collecting, the more it pays off later.

  5. Cheap Manastones everywhere

    Fill every slot with low-grade stones now. Keep your best rolls for gold pieces you won't replace soon; changing gear loses the stones.

  6. Soul Binding on keepers

    After level 45, bind only gold gear you plan to keep. Costs climb each step, up to about 632,000 Kinah at step 10, and bound items can't be traded.

  7. Switch focus to CP around 2,100 item level

    Once you can enter the late dungeons, Soul Binding and better Manastones raise CP faster than chasing item level. Item level keeps rising as you farm.

Mistakes that waste materials

  • Watching item level only. If your item level is high and your CP is low, the gap is in Daevanion, Stigmas or pets, not gear.
  • Enhancing gear you're about to replace. Keep leveling gear at low enhancement. Use Gear Transfer to move enhancement to a better piece of the same type.
  • Pushing past +10 too early. From +11, attempts can fail and cost climbs fast. Do it only on pieces you'll keep for a long time.
  • Putting your best Manastones on temporary gear. They are lost when the piece is replaced or extracted.
  • Leveling every Stigma. Shards are limited. Only level the ones in your setup.
  • Letting Odyle Energy cap. Anything over the cap is lost.

NCSOFT hasn't published exact CP formulas or every success rate for the global version. Numbers here come from community testing and may change with patches. Check the in-game preview before risky upgrades.
