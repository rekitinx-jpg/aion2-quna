Arcana

Arcana are cards you slot into five dedicated slots. A full set of Unique cards adds 400 item level, more than almost anything else in the game, plus stats and skill levels. They drop in Transcendence, which opens at 1,600 item level.

Slots and where they drop

Each slot takes only its own card type. Every slot has a Vigor and a Magic version of each card.

Higher Transcendence stages drop better grades. Unique cards start dropping at stage 3, except the Unique Chalice, which only drops at stage 4. Stage entry: 1,600 / 1,900 / 2,200 / 2,500 item level (see the item level roadmap).

Vigor or Magic?

Primal Vigor · damage dealers

2 cards: +60 PvE Attack while HP is 70% or higher.
4 cards: +150 PvE Attack while HP is 70% or higher.

Magic Armor · tanks, healers, MP-heavy casters

2 cards: restores 1,500 MP when you drop to 20% MP (30 s cooldown).
4 cards: +1,000 PvE Defense while MP is 50% or higher.

The names are misleading. Vigor doesn't mean physical and Magic doesn't mean magical. Magic cards carry Attack and Critical Hit; Vigor cards carry Combat Speed and cooldown reduction. Pick by the set bonus and the stats, not the name. Decide early so your 2-card bonus is active from the first stage.

Enhancing, extracting and Transmute

Enhancing

Feed spare cards or Training Arcana into a card. It fills an EXP bar, so it never fails and never loses levels. Each level adds +1 item level and +1 to a random skill line.

EXP to max: Common 900 · Rare 4,200 · Epic 18,000 · Unique 93,000. Training Arcana give 200 to 5,000 EXP.

Extract and Transmute

Extract duplicate cards into Mysterious Crystals. Transmute lets you pick the set, slot and grade you want, and lock skill lines for an extra cost. It's expensive, so save it for slots that won't drop for you.

Reported cost for a Unique card: 5 crystals + 2,000,000 Kinah, plus 10 crystals + 3,000,000 Kinah per locked line. Transmute changes each season.

Priorities

  1. Fill all five slots right away

    Even Common cards are +20 item level each. An empty slot is the most expensive mistake.

  2. Pick Vigor or Magic

    Aim for the 2-card bonus first, then 4.

  3. Chalice is the big one

    It's the only slot that can roll any of your class's skills, so it's the single biggest upgrade, and the best target for Transmute.

  4. Enhance only keepers

    Sources disagree on whether all invested EXP carries over when you feed a card, so don't pour EXP into cards you'll replace soon.

  5. Push stages 3 and 4

    That's where Unique cards drop. Plan your Daevanion board with your Arcana so you don't double up on the same skills.
