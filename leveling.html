Leveling 1 to 45

The main story quests carry you almost the whole way: about 97% of the experience to 45 for Elyos and 99% for Asmodians. Follow the gold story quests, kill what's on your path, and only stop for side quests when the story makes you wait. A first character usually takes about 4–6 hours of play.

Sealed dungeons are worth it

On your own faction's map

Each one gives 15,000 Kinah, Enhance Stones, 2 Daevanion Crystals, Wisdom Stones and a title. There are dozens per map from level 10 to 45.

Strongholds

Give belt enhancement scrolls. Pick them up while you're in the area; you'll need them for the belt upgrade path.

Tips that save hours

  1. Never skip a gold story quest

    They give almost all the experience. Regional quests below 45 give under 2% of it, so save those for 45.

  2. Don't over-invest in leveling gear

    You'll replace it within hours. Enhance the weapon to +10 (it can't fail), and take the amulet and belt to +10 so you can morph them. Keep good Manastones for later.

  3. Keep gold items

    Don't sell or salvage Unique (gold) pieces you pick up; they're useful later.

  4. Spend points right away

    Daevanion and skill points do nothing while they sit unspent.

  5. Activate every Kibelisk

    Return Scrolls can only take you to ones you've activated.

  6. Skip dungeon end chests while leveling

    Opening them costs Odyle Energy for gear you'll soon replace. Save the energy for 45.

Reported bug: Duty Missions may stay hidden until the day after you reach 45. If the Duty tab looks empty, check again after the next daily reset.
