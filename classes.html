Guide · All 8 classes · Updated Oct 14, 2026

Pick your class

Every class is open to both Elyos and Asmodians. You can't change class on an existing character, so this choice sticks. Pick what you want to do most, and the list narrows to the classes that suit it.

What do you want to do?

Strengths here are where guides agree, not a fixed tier list. Balance changes with patches. Difficulty dots: more gold = harder to play well.

Choosing well

  • Groups always need Templars, Clerics and Chanters. They're the only pure tank, the only pure healer and the strongest party support, so you'll find parties fastest on these.
  • Damage classes have more competition for party spots, but they level and farm alone more comfortably.
  • Try before you commit. The first few levels are quick. Make two or three characters and play each up to level 10.
  • Skins are per weapon type. Weapon skins in the shop are locked to a class's weapon, so check the skins list for your class.
