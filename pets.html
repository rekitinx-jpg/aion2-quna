Pets & Genus Insight

Every pet you own gives stats, even when it isn't out, and every new pet feeds the Genus Insight boards that roll extra stats for your character. Collecting many different pets beats maxing one favourite. Pets and Genus Insight are shared by all your characters on the same server, so alts can farm souls for your main.

A new pet is worth twice as much Genus Insight EXP as levelling one, which is why unlocking many pets comes first. Korea and Taiwan allow pet level 5; global stops at 3.

The five genera

Every pet in a genus gives the same owned effect at level 3. Cogni and Fera lean offensive, Natura and Varian defensive.

How Genus Insight works

Boards

One board per genus. Each levels from 1 to 10 and has 9 stat slots: levels 1–9 each open one slot, and level 10 raises the odds of top grades. Each board has 3 presets that share a level but keep separate stats, so you can keep PvE and PvP setups.

Analysis (reroll)

Costs 4 Soul Crystals of that genus and 1,000 Kinah with nothing locked. It rerolls every unlocked slot and gives 100 EXP per rerolled slot. Locking a slot costs more and that slot gives no EXP.

Heroic odds

Heroic stats can only appear from board level 9 (1%), rising to 5% at level 10. Level 10 odds are reported as Common 20%, Rare 30%, Epic 30%, Unique 15%, Heroic 5%.

Don't lock anything before level 10. Every lock makes each reroll cost more and earns less EXP. Level the board first, then lock the good rolls.

Which stats to keep

  • Offence first: Double Chance, Attack Bonus, Boss Attack, Critical Hit and Accuracy Bonus are the rolls guides rank highest.
  • Then defence: Defense Bonus and Evasion for tanks and PvP.
  • Avoid genus-specific lines such as "Cogni Accuracy". They only work against monsters of that genus.

Getting souls and crystals

  1. Start with your first pet

    A story quest around level 10–11 lets you pick one of three Fera pets: Fossa, Armadon or Tog.

  2. Farm common, low-level monsters

    Souls drop from monsters of each species. Species with many low-level spawns are the quickest new unlocks. The pet menu's Source tab shows where each soul drops.

  3. Check the other sources

    The Black Cloud Merchant (rotating stock), Hidden Cubes, chests, events and the Daeva Pass all give souls.

  4. Collect Soul Crystals for rerolls

    They come from extra souls of maxed pets, Duty Missions, Hidden Cubes, collection milestones and the Shugo Festival shop.

  5. Save rainbow crystals for the Special board

    Rainbow Soul Crystals work on any board. Special pets can't be farmed in the world, so their board only levels through Analysis.

Mounts, Transform and auto-loot

  • Your equipped pet is your mount, and all mounts move at the same speed, so pick the one you like.
  • Pet Transform turns you into a level 3 pet for 5 minutes using one Empathy Candy, from collection milestones and the Shugo Festival shop. It's a visual and movement change, not a combat buff.
  • Auto-loot needs a Pet: Auto-Loot Voucher. Without one, you pick up drops yourself.
