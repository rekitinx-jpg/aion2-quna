Stigmas

Stigmas are four extra skills that grow stronger as you level them with Stigma Shards. Shards are limited, so the real decisions are which four you slot and which one you push first.

4 slots on global75 shards to reach level 20Resets refund shards

How Stigmas work

Slots

Open at character levels 22, 27, 32 and 37. Global has 4 slots; Korea and Taiwan have 6.

Breakpoints

New effects unlock automatically at Stigma levels 5, 10, 15 and 20. Levels in between add little, so push one Stigma to its next breakpoint at a time.

Max level

Level 20 is the practical cap in global Season 1. The client shows 25, but past 20 needs Superior Stigma Shards, which are very scarce.

Resetting a Stigma refunds its shards for a Kinah fee, so it's safe to move shards around after 45. You can't swap Stigmas in combat.
