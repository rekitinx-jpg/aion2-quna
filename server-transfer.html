Server transfer

For two weeks you can move your characters to another server for free. You only get one transfer per account and it can't be undone, so check the rules below before you buy it.

Transfer period: Oct 14 – Oct 28

When and how much

Week 1 · Early Access servers only

From the maintenance on Oct 13 (PDT) / Oct 14 (CEST) until the maintenance on Oct 20 / Oct 21.

Week 2 · Launch servers only

From the maintenance on Oct 20 (PDT) / Oct 21 (CEST) until the maintenance on Oct 27 / Oct 28.

CostPriceLimit

Example from the notice: moving 6 characters to a server where you have only 4 free slots costs 2,000 Quna for the 2 extra slots. Buy it in game under In-Game Shop → Server Transfer.

Before you buy: checklist

Rules

  • Same faction only. Elyos can't move to an Asmodian server or the reverse.
  • Same region only.
  • Early Access servers can only move to other Early Access servers.
  • One transfer per account, from one server to one server.
  • Can't be cancelled or reversed once it starts.
  • Fails if either server has hit its transfer limit.

Your character must

  • Be level 10 or higher.
  • Be standing in a village, not in the Abyss, a Spacetime Rift or a dungeon.
  • Not be in a Legion or have a Legion application pending.
  • Have no active Duty Missions.
  • Have no Market listings, unsold items or uncollected sales.
  • Have nothing waiting in Shop Storage (including Founder's Pack) or the mailbox.

Empty your Server Storage first. Items and Kina in Server Storage do not transfer. Move anything you want to keep into the character's inventory or Character Storage before transferring.

What moves with you

✓ Daevanion, skills, Stigmas, Arcana, wingsAll character progress moves.
✓ Titles, achievements, Closet skins, pets, TheostonesCombined with the new server's collection and removed from the old one.

✗ Server Storage itemsOnly the number of storage expansion slots carries over.
✗ Mail block listStarts empty on the new server.
✗ Your character nameYou get a temporary name and choose a new one at first login. If your old name is taken there, you can't use it.

Duplicate shop pets or skins become reward tickets you can exchange in the shop or refund through support, valid until the Nov 24 (PST) / Nov 25 (CET) maintenance.
